The Moon finally answers back — one line, 181 characters, in the first person, after four billion years of wishes, howls and 3 a.m. promises:

> "You wished on me for four billion years — for love, for money, for your ex to text back — and not once did anyone wish me anything. So tonight the wish is mine: I'm calling in sick."

It wins because it answers the exact thing the Moon has been asked for — love, money, an ex texting back — with a grievance only the Moon could hold, then flips the whole relationship halfway through: nobody ever wished *it* anything, so tonight the wish is the Moon's, and the Moon is taking the night off. Wry, old, tired of being wished upon, and the turn lands before the line ends.
